Frequently asked questions

Does Aether diagnose medical or mental health conditions?

No. Aether is a reflective wellness tool, not a medical or mental health service. If you mention illness or a diagnosis, the conversation will gently point you toward a qualified doctor or therapist.

Is the Janampatri astronomically accurate?

In this version, your reflection chart is a realistic placeholder generated consistently from your birth details — it's meant to spark reflection, not serve as precise astrology.

How are recommendations decided?

Your conversation is scored transparently against each modality's typical themes. You'll always see a confidence score and the specific reasons behind every suggestion.

Are practitioners vetted?

Every practitioner listed includes their experience, specialization, languages and rating so you can make an informed choice before booking.
